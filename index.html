What DL X covers

Four kinds of work.

The division exists for the things that don't fit a client project: ideas worth testing, tools worth building and hardware worth owning outright.

Experimental prototypes

Built to answer a question rather than fill an order. Most stay on the bench — the point is finding out.

Proprietary products

Designs the lab owns end to end, manufactured in-house and sold direct, without a middleman between design and buyer.

Strategic partnerships

Joint development where DL X brings design capability, tooling and short-run manufacturing to someone else's idea.

Future technologies

Longer-horizon work with no client attached. The part of the lab that has to stay curious to stay useful.

How it gets made

Printed after you order it.

There is no warehouse behind this shop. Orders go into the next print batch, which is why lead times are stated in days rather than hidden, and why colour or engraving variants are a conversation rather than a separate SKU.

It also means the catalogue can stay honest: a product is listed when the geometry is validated and the tolerances are known, not when a container arrives.
