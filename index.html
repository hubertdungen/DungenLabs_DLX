Materials & capabilities

Three processes, and what each one is for.

Every part here is made in house. Enclosed extrusion covers engineering polymers and fibre-reinforced filaments; resin covers the detail work that extrusion cannot reach. Which process a part goes through is a design decision, not a preference — it follows from what the part has to survive.

Extrusion · heated chamber

Engineering polymers

An actively heated, enclosed chamber and a high-temperature hotend are what make ABS, ASA, PC and nylon behave instead of warping off the plate. This is where anything structural, outdoor or heat-exposed gets made.

Envelope
Up to 300 mm cubed
Materials
ABS, ASA, PC, PA, PETG
Layer range
0.1 – 0.3 mm
Strength
Highest available here

Extrusion · hardened

Fibre-reinforced & multi-colour

A hardened flow path takes abrasive carbon- and glass-filled filament, for parts that must stay dimensionally honest under load. Multi-material capability puts colour and markings into the geometry rather than printing them on afterwards.

Envelope
Up to 250 mm cubed
Materials
CF and GF filled, TPU, PLA
Colour
Multi-material in one part
Best for
Jigs and fixtures that must not flex

MSLA · resin

Fine detail

Masked stereolithography for fine features, thin walls and surfaces that come off the plate already smooth — enclosures for small electronics, fit models, presentation parts. Detail over strength, deliberately.

Process
405 nm masked SLA
Layers
From 0.01 mm
Materials
Standard, tough and casting resins
Best for
Resolution extrusion cannot reach

Materials in regular use

Materials DL X prints, with their properties and typical applications
MaterialWhy it gets chosenTypical use
PETG Tough, dimensionally stable, resists IPA and most workshop chemicals The default for functional parts and enclosures
ASA UV-stable and heat-tolerant; will not go chalky outdoors Anything living outside, or near warm air
ABS Impact-resistant, solvent-smoothable, machines cleanly Housings that get handled and dropped
PLA Highest detail and the least fuss, but softens with heat Fit checks, jigs, display parts
TPU Flexible and abrasion-resistant Gaskets, bumpers, strain reliefs
PC High temperature resistance and real structural strength Load-bearing brackets, hot environments
Nylon (PA) Low friction and fatigue-resistant Bearings, living hinges, moving assemblies
Carbon-filled (CF / GF) Stiff and dimensionally stable, at the cost of a hardened nozzle Fixtures and jigs that must not flex
Resin Feature resolution extrusion cannot reach Small enclosures, fine detail, presentation models
