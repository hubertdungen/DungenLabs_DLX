2. Products and prices

Products are 3D-printed after you order them. Photos show the design; layer lines and small colour differences between batches are normal for the process. 3. How the contract is made

Choosing Pay by card or Pay with crypto is an order with an obligation to pay. The contract is concluded when the payment is confirmed, and you receive a confirmation by email. Orders through the quote form become binding only once you accept the quote and pay.

4. Payment

Card payments are processed by Stripe; crypto payments by NOWPayments. Card details never reach this site.

5. Production and delivery

Made-to-order items are dispatched within 3–5 working days, plus the carrier's transit time. We deliver to Portugal and the rest of the European Union. If an order cannot be delivered within 30 days, you may cancel it and are refunded in full.

6. Right of withdrawal (14 days)

You can withdraw from the purchase of catalogue items within 14 days of receiving them, without giving a reason. To do so, send a clear statement to info@dungenlabs.com with your order reference, then return the items within 14 days of that message. The direct cost of returning the items is yours.

We refund everything you paid, including the original standard shipping, within 14 days of receiving your withdrawal, using the same payment method. We may hold the refund until the items are back or you show proof of having sent them. You are responsible for any loss of value caused by handling the items beyond what is needed to check them.

Exception: the right of withdrawal does not apply to items made to your specifications or clearly personalised — custom colours, dimensions, engraving or any variant quoted individually (article 17(1)(c) of Decreto-Lei 24/2014). This is stated in the quote before you accept it.

7. Legal guarantee

All items carry the legal guarantee of conformity of 3 years from delivery. If an item is defective or not as described, write to info@dungenlabs.com and it is repaired, replaced or refunded, return shipping included. The guarantee does not cover normal wear, misuse, or heat deformation from leaving parts in direct sun or in a hot car.

8. Complaints and disputes

Complaints can be made by email or in the Livro de Reclamações Eletrónico. Consumers may also turn to an alternative dispute resolution entity:

Centro de Arbitragem de Conflitos de Consumo de Lisboa — centroarbitragemlisboa.pt
CNIACC — Centro Nacional de Informação e Arbitragem de Conflitos de Consumo — cniacc.pt

More information on the Portal do Consumidor: consumidor.gov.pt.
