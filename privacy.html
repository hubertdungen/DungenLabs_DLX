Privacy policy.

What personal data DL X receives through this shop, why, who processes it on our behalf, how long it is kept and how you can exercise your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Versão portuguesa abaixo.

Last updated: 20 October 2026

1. Who is responsible

DL X is a division of DungenLabs, the trade name of Hubert José Pires Van Den Dungen, a sole trader registered in Portugal (NIF 269031049) operating in Lisbon, who is the controller of the personal data described here. Full identification is in the legal notice. For any privacy question or request, write to info@dungenlabs.com.

2. What we collect and why

Orders paid by card

Card payments are taken on Stripe's checkout page. We receive your name, email address, delivery address and what you ordered; we never see your card details. We use this to make and ship the order, send the order confirmation and tracking, and keep the records the law requires. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and legal obligations such as invoicing (Article 6(1)(c)).

Orders paid in crypto

Crypto payments run on NOWPayments. We receive the order reference, amount and payment status; you send us your delivery address by email after paying. Legal basis: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)).

Quote requests

The order form sends us your name, email address, country, the product, quantity, colour and any notes. We use it to reply and prepare the order. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Article 6(1)(b)).

Email

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, and use it to answer you (Article 6(1)(b) and (f)).

Technical data

To deliver and protect the website, our hosting provider processes technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in operating a secure website (Article 6(1)(f)).

3. Cookies and browser storage

This shop sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Fonts and images are served from our own domain. Your cart and language choice are kept in your browser's local storage so they survive a page reload; they stay on your device and are only sent to us when you go to checkout. Stripe's and NOWPayments' payment pages are run by them and apply their own cookie policies.

4. Who processes the data

  • Netlify, Inc. (United States) — hosting, the order form and the checkout functions, as our processor.
  • Stripe Payments Europe, Ltd. (Ireland) — card payments. Stripe also acts as an independent controller for fraud prevention and its legal obligations as a payment provider; see stripe.com/privacy.
  • NOWPayments — crypto payments, under its own privacy policy.
  • Resend, Inc. (United States) — sending the order confirmation emails, as our processor.
  • Carriers (for example CTT) — your name and delivery address, to deliver the parcel.

Transfers to the United States are covered by the providers' data processing agreements, which incorporate the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell or rent personal data.

5. How long we keep it

Order and invoicing records are kept for 10 years, as Portuguese accounting and tax law requires. Quote requests and emails that do not lead to an order are deleted within 12 months of our last contact.

6. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Send your request to info@dungenlabs.com; we reply within one month. Records the law obliges us to keep cannot be deleted before that period ends. You can also lodge a complaint with the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (cnpd.pt).

7. Security

The site is served only over HTTPS, payment details never reach this site, and access to orders is limited to the people who handle them.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
