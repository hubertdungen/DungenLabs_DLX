Shipping & Returns

How DL X orders are made and sent.

Almost everything here is printed after you order it. That keeps the catalogue wide and the waste low, but it does change the timings and the return conditions compared to a warehouse shop.

Production time

  • Made-to-order items: 3–5 working days before dispatch.
  • In-stock items: dispatched within 1 working day.
  • Custom colours, engraving or variants: quoted individually.

Shipping

  • Orders ship from Lisbon, Portugal.
  • Portugal: €4.90 tracked — free on orders over €30.
  • Elsewhere in the EU: €11.90 tracked.
  • Shipping is charged once per order, not per item, however many pieces are in the parcel.
  • Crypto orders are charged the EU rate up front, because no address is collected before payment. If you are in Portugal, or the order qualifies for free delivery, the difference is refunded once the address is known.
  • Tracking is emailed as soon as the parcel is handed over.

Returns

If an item arrives damaged, defective or not as described, contact info@dungenlabs.com within 14 days of delivery and it will be replaced or refunded, return shipping included.

You can also withdraw from the purchase of catalogue items within 14 days of delivery, without giving a reason — the return postage is yours and the refund includes the original shipping. Items made to your specification (custom colours, dimensions or engraving) are excluded. Details in the conditions of sale.

Warranty

Every item carries the 3-year legal guarantee of conformity under Portuguese law. Normal wear, damage from misuse, and heat deformation from leaving parts in direct sun or in a car are not covered.

Contact

Questions about an order, a lead time or a custom variant: info@dungenlabs.com.
